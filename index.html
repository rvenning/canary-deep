📖 How it works

It is minesweeper, but no board here ever needs a guess. Before you are dealt one, a solver plays it through using logic alone — if it would ever come down to a coin toss, that board is thrown away and another is cut. Every square you are stuck on can be worked out.

  • Tap a square to dig it. Press, slide to correct yourself, then lift — nothing happens until you let go.
  • 🚩 Flag a square you think holds gas, three ways: tap the Flag half of the switch under the board and then tap squares, or hold a square down without switching, or right click on a mouse. Tap a flag again to lift it.
  • Tap a number you have fully flagged to clear everything else around it in one go — this is where the speed comes from.
  • 🐤 The bird does one of two jobs. Send it ahead and it lands on a square you could already have proved safe. Keep it in the cage and it takes the hit the first time you tap gas, costing you 15 seconds instead of the shift.
  • Stars: one for getting out, two for beating par, three for beating par with the cage still full.
